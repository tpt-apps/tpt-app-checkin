## Description

**Run check-in for your event without paying a per-event SaaS fee — and without needing WiFi at the venue.**

TPT Check-in is an offline QR ticket scanner: import your ticket list, scan attendees in with your phone or tablet camera, and track who's checked in — all running locally on the device, with no internet connection required once it's loaded.

- **Import your ticket list** — paste ticket IDs or upload a CSV
- **Live camera scanning** — point your phone at a QR ticket, get instant check-in confirmation
- **Duplicate detection** — flags tickets that have already been scanned
- **Live attendance dashboard** — total, checked-in, remaining, searchable ticket table
- **100% offline** — once installed, use it at any venue with no signal
- **Export attendance CSV** — back up or reconcile attendance after the event
- **No account, no upload, no ongoing subscription** — your ticket list and attendance data never leave the device

### Who it's for
Event organizers, meetup/conference hosts, and venues who want simple, reliable check-in without a subscription or an internet dependency at the door.

### How it's delivered
Your purchase is a ZIP download containing:
- **The Windows installer** (`.msi`/`.exe`) — double-click to install, run it at the door like any desktop app. No internet connection needed after install.
- **A self-host bundle** for phone/tablet use — drag one folder onto a free instant-hosting page (steps included in the download) to get it on staff phones for door scanning, installable to their homescreen.

Nothing about this product depends on any ongoing service from us — once installed (or self-hosted), it's yours to keep working indefinitely.

## FAQ

**Does this need WiFi at the venue?**
No — after installing, it works fully offline. This is the whole point.

**Can multiple people scan at once?**
Yes — install the desktop app or the self-hosted phone version on each staff member's device; each tracks its own local copy, then export each device's CSV and merge at the end of the event (a full multi-device live sync is a possible future upgrade, not included in v1).

**What if I generate tickets elsewhere?**
Any QR code containing a unique ticket ID text string will work — export your ticket IDs as a CSV from your ticketing/registration tool.

**Is attendance data safe if I close the app?**
Yes — it's stored locally in the browser (IndexedDB), so it persists between sessions on the same device.
