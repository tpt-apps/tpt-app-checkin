# Privacy — TPT Check-in

TPT Check-in runs entirely on your device (WebAssembly + IndexedDB).

- **No account, no login.**
- **No data upload.** Your ticket list and attendance records are stored only in this browser's local IndexedDB, on this device. Nothing is sent to any server.
- **No telemetry or analytics.**
- **Camera access** is used only to scan tickets in real time — no frames are stored or transmitted anywhere.
- **Works offline.** Once the app has loaded once, it keeps working with no internet connection at all (it's an installable Progressive Web App).
- **Your data, your backup.** Use the "Export attendance CSV" button to save a copy of attendance at any time — that's the only way data leaves the device, and only when you choose to.
