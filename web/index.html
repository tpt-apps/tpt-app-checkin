About TPT Check-in

TPT Check-in is an offline event ticket scanner by TPT Solutions.

Privacy: your ticket list and attendance data are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB), on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Once loaded, the app keeps working with no internet connection — export a CSV at the end of your event to back up attendance.

Questions or feedback: visit tptsolutions.co.nz.
